Reverse Engineering & Machine Repair

When a critical machine component fails and the original manufacturer no longer supports it , or when lead times stretch to months you simply do not have , we step in. HPS Technologies specialises in reverse engineering failed, obsolete, or discontinued components from physical samples, delivering accurate replacement parts that restore your production line fast.
We respond to emergency assessments within 24 hours and deliver replacement component drawings within three to five working days.

On the embedded systems side, we develop firmware in C and C++ for STM32, ESP32, Arduino, and PIC microcontrollers, and implement FPGA designs for applications requiring deterministic real-time processing. We specialise in IoT device development across WiFi, Bluetooth, LoRa, and Zigbee protocols, wireless sensor networks for industrial condition monitoring, motor control systems, and custom communication interfaces including UART, SPI, I2C, CAN, and Modbus.

Mechanical Design & 3D CAD

Precise mechanical design is the foundation of every reliable product. HPS Technologies provides comprehensive 3D CAD modelling, mechanical assembly design, and manufacturing documentation services that take your concept from initial sketch to a complete, production-ready drawing package.
We work in SolidWorks and CATIA across a wide range of mechanical applications including industrial machinery components, enclosures and housings, structural frames and weldments, sheet metal parts and fabrications, mechanisms and linkage systems, and precision fixtures and jigs.

HPS Technologies provides independent technical advisory services to startups validating a new product concept, established companies entering an unfamiliar technology area, and R&D managers who need a credible second opinion before presenting to their board or investors. Our consulting services include technical feasibility studies, concept design and technology selection, engineering risk assessment, independent design review and peer validation, prototype strategy and development roadmap planning, material and manufacturing process selection, and supplier evaluation.
