Advanced Analysis

Beyond CFD — Structural & Coupled Simulation


Our experts extend beyond fluid flow into structural integrity and multi-physics coupling ,giving you a complete picture of your system's behavior.

FEA

Finite Element Analysis

Structural simulation to evaluate stress, strain, deformation, fatigue life, and failure risk in mechanical components , before you build a single prototype.

  • Static & dynamic structural analysis

  • Modal & harmonic response

  • Fatigue life prediction

  • Nonlinear material behavior

  • Thermal stress analysis

  • Buckling & stability studies

  • Composite & weld joint assessment

FSI

Fluid–Structure Interaction

Coupled simulation where fluid forces deform structures and structural deformations feed back into the flow , essential for aeroelasticity, hydroelasticity, and pressure vessel design.

  • One-way & two-way FSI coupling

  • Aeroelastic flutter analysis

  • Hydroelastic ship hull behavior

  • Valve & seal deformation

  • Flexible pipe & hose simulation

  • Blood flow in medical devices (bioFSI)

  • Wind-induced vibration studies
